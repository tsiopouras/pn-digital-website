About P&N Digital

P&N Digital is a web development agency based in Cyprus. We build websites that give local businesses a clear, trustworthy online presence.

How we work

  • Honest content.We publish only information the business has confirmed, and we say so when something is a placeholder.
  • A design for each business.We design around the actual business and its customers, not a template.
  • Plain communication.We explain choices in simple terms and agree scope, content and price in advance.

Our process

  1. Brief

    We confirm what the business does, who it serves and which details are verified.

  2. Direction

    We propose a design direction and agree it before the build starts.

  3. Build

    A static, responsive site, checked at phone, tablet and desktop sizes.

  4. Handover

    You approve the content, then we agree publishing, hosting and support.
