Process

From first conversation to launch.

  1. Initial conversation

    We talk about your business, your customers and what you want the site to do.

  2. Understanding the business

    We check the details you have confirmed and list anything still missing.

  3. Planning and design

    We propose a direction, agree the scope and the price, then design it.

  4. Development

    We build a fast, responsive site and test it on real screen sizes.

  5. Review and refinements

    You review every page. We make the changes you agreed.

  6. Launch and handover

    We agree how the site goes live, and explain how to keep it up to date.

Language guarantee

Your website in any language your customers speak.

Greek, English, Russian, Ukrainian, Hebrew, Arabic, Turkish, German, French, Chinese or any other language: if it can be shown on the web, we can build your website in it. That includes right-to-left languages such as Hebrew and Arabic, with the whole layout mirrored properly.

Every language you choose gets:

  • Its own pages, menus and buttons, not an automatic translation plugin
  • Search engine setup for each language, so Google shows people the right version
  • A language switcher on every page
  • A reply in the visitor’s own language from the AI assistant, if your site has one
  • A review of every translation with you before anything goes live
