Project 03, Professional services Working demo

Nexora Business Solutions

A minimalist corporate website with confident typography and clear calls to action.

A demonstration website for a fictional business consultancy, in English and Greek. It has a full homepage, detailed services, illustrative case studies, an FAQ and a seven-step enquiry walkthrough with a live summary. Nothing is presented as real: there are no clients, certifications or measured results, and nothing is sent or stored.

The full website is not available to open yet. This page will link to it once it is finished and has a working address.

What it includes

  • Full homepage: value proposition, services, industries, process, why choose us, illustrative case studies, labelled sample testimonials and an 8-question FAQ
  • Detailed services page with benefits and illustrative deliverables
  • Seven-step enquiry walkthrough with validation, back and edit controls, a live summary and a demo-only confirmation
  • English and Greek with a working language switcher; it also follows the language of this site when previewed

Design direction

Colour
Warm paper, deep navy and a restrained brass accent
Type
Clean, highly readable sans-serif
Mood
Calm, credible and precise.

Made for

Consultancies, accountants and other professional service firms.

Behind the build

How we built it

No templates and no page builders. The layout, styles and every interaction were written from scratch, then tested until they held up on every screen.

  • 45KB of custom code
  • 138CSS rules
  • 54JavaScript functions
  • 181Greek text strings
  • 6screen sizes tested
  1. 01

    The brief

    Business owners want proof before they get in touch: what the firm does, how it works and what a project looks like, without exaggerated claims.

  2. 02

    Structure

    We organised services, process and illustrative case studies, then planned a seven-step enquiry with a live summary that never pretends to send anything.

  3. 03

    Design system

    Warm paper, deep navy and a restrained brass accent, with a clean, highly readable sans-serif, for a calm, credible and precise feel.

  4. 04

    The build

    Detailed services, case studies with clearly labelled sample figures, an 8-question FAQ, charts drawn in code and the enquiry walkthrough with checks at every step, all written from scratch.

  5. 05

    Two languages and AI

    Built in English and Greek from day one, 181 Greek text strings, with an AI assistant that matches a visitor’s needs to the right service.

  6. 06

    Testing

    Checked in a real browser at 6 screen sizes from 375 to 1920 pixels, with a keyboard only, with animations switched off, and in Greek with an automatic check for any English left behind.

The numbers are measured from the demo’s own code and our test runs.

Fictional brand. Names, prices and details are invented, and no form on the demo sends anything.
