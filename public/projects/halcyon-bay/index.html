Project 04, Hotel Working demo

Halcyon Bay

A calm seaside hotel website with an availability calendar and a booking flow that prices your stay.

A demonstration website for a fictional seaside hotel. It has a full homepage, rooms you can filter and open for details, an experiences page, a gallery, an FAQ and an eight-step booking walkthrough with a date calendar that adds up the room, meals and extras as you choose. Illustrations stand in for photography, and nothing is actually booked or sent.

Open full website

The full website is not available to open yet. This page will link to it once it is finished and has a working address.

What it includes

  • Full homepage: story, featured rooms, amenities, a gallery with enlarged views, sample testimonials, arrival and departure times and a 10-question FAQ
  • Rooms page with view and feature filters and a details pop-up for every room
  • Experiences page with extras you can add to a stay, and a sample day
  • Eight-step booking walkthrough: a date calendar (stays of up to 14 nights), guests, only the rooms that fit your group, meal plans, extras, a live price summary, validation, back and edit controls and a demo-only confirmation
  • English and Greek with a working language switcher; it also follows the language of this site when previewed

Design direction

Colour
Warm sand and whitewash with deep sea blue and a terracotta accent
Type
Classic serif headlines with italic accents
Mood
Calm, warm and unhurried.

Made for

Hotels, guesthouses, holiday rentals and other places to stay.

Behind the build

How we built it

No templates and no page builders. The layout, styles and every interaction were written from scratch, then tested until they held up on every screen.

  • 86KB of custom code
  • 221CSS rules
  • 97JavaScript functions
  • 327Greek text strings
  • 6screen sizes tested
  1. 01

    The brief

    Guests compare rooms, check dates and want to know the full price before they commit. The site had to make all three easy on a phone.

  2. 02

    Structure

    We planned the booking first: dates, guests and a room that fits, then meals and extras, with the total adding up as you choose. The pages were built around it.

  3. 03

    Design system

    Warm sand, whitewash and deep sea blue with a terracotta accent and classic serif headlines, for a calm, unhurried feel.

  4. 04

    The build

    A date calendar for stays of up to 14 nights, room filters and pop-ups, a gallery, extras, a 10-question FAQ and the booking flow with clear availability rules, all written from scratch.

  5. 05

    Two languages and AI

    327 Greek text strings, and an AI assistant that knows the rooms, meal plans and extras and points families to the right room.

  6. 06

    Testing

    Checked in a real browser at 6 screen sizes from 375 to 1920 pixels, with a keyboard only, with animations switched off, and in Greek with an automatic check for any English left behind.

The numbers are measured from the demo’s own code and our test runs.

Fictional brand. Names, prices and details are invented, and no form on the demo sends anything.
