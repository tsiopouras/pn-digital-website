Project 01, Restaurant Working demo

Olive & Oak

A Mediterranean restaurant website led by food photography and elegant typography.

A demonstration website for a fictional premium Mediterranean restaurant in Cyprus. It has a full homepage, an 18-dish menu with category and dietary filters, an eight-step reservation walkthrough and an FAQ. Illustrations stand in for photography, and nothing is actually booked or sent.

Open full website

The full website is not available to open yet. This page will link to it once it is finished and has a working address.

What it includes

  • Full homepage: story, signature dishes, chef’s recommendations, seasonal specials, gallery, sample testimonials, hours and an FAQ
  • Menu with 18 dishes, category navigation, vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free filters, and dish detail pop-ups
  • Eight-step reservation walkthrough with validation, back and edit controls, a review and a demo-only confirmation
  • Animated FAQ accordion, mobile menu and scroll animations
  • English and Greek with a working language switcher; it also follows the language of this site when previewed

Design direction

Colour
Deep charcoal, warm ivory, muted olive green and subtle bronze
Type
Elegant serif headings with a clean sans-serif body
Mood
Warm and unhurried.

Made for

Restaurants, cafés and bakeries.

Behind the build

How we built it

No templates and no page builders. The layout, styles and every interaction were written from scratch, then tested until they held up on every screen.

  • 57KB of custom code
  • 135CSS rules
  • 65JavaScript functions
  • 234Greek text strings
  • 6screen sizes tested
  1. 01

    The brief

    Who is it for? People deciding where to eat tonight, mostly on a phone. The site had to answer three questions fast: what’s on the menu, when is it open, and how do I book?

  2. 02

    Structure

    We mapped every page and the reservation journey step by step, eight steps from choosing a date to a review screen, before writing a line of code.

  3. 03

    Design system

    Charcoal, ivory, olive and bronze, an elegant serif for headings and a clean sans-serif for reading, chosen for a warm, unhurried mood.

  4. 04

    The build

    18 dishes with category and dietary filters and pop-up details, the reservation flow with checks at every step, FAQ, gallery and scroll animations, all written from scratch.

  5. 05

    Two languages and AI

    234 Greek text strings, dates and prices written the Greek way, and an AI assistant set up with the menu and opening hours.

  6. 06

    Testing

    Checked in a real browser at 6 screen sizes from 375 to 1920 pixels, with a keyboard only, with animations switched off, and in Greek with an automatic check for any English left behind.

The numbers are measured from the demo’s own code and our test runs.

Fictional brand. Names, prices and details are invented, and no form on the demo sends anything.
