Project 02, Barbershop Working demo

THE CUT

An editorial barbershop website with bold typography and a services price list.

A demonstration website for a fictional barbershop. It has a full homepage, services with prices and durations, barber profiles, a before-and-after slider, an FAQ and an eight-step booking walkthrough that adds up price and time as you choose. Illustrations stand in for photography, and nothing is actually booked or sent.

Open full website

The full website is not available to open yet. This page will link to it once it is finished and has a working address.

What it includes

  • Full homepage: story, featured services, signature haircuts, barbers, before-and-after slider, sample testimonials, hours and a 10-question FAQ
  • Services page with prices, durations and add-ons
  • Barber profiles (fictional) with a “book with” shortcut
  • Eight-step booking walkthrough with a live price and duration summary, date and time rules, validation, back and edit controls and a demo-only confirmation
  • English and Greek with a working language switcher; it also follows the language of this site when previewed

Design direction

Colour
Black and white with a restrained red accent
Type
Strong, high-contrast headlines
Mood
Bold and confident.

Made for

Barbershops, salons and other appointment-led local businesses.

Behind the build

How we built it

No templates and no page builders. The layout, styles and every interaction were written from scratch, then tested until they held up on every screen.

  • 55KB of custom code
  • 149CSS rules
  • 68JavaScript functions
  • 214Greek text strings
  • 6screen sizes tested
  1. 01

    The brief

    Customers want to know three things: what it costs, how long it takes and who is free. Everything is built around a quick, clear booking.

  2. 02

    Structure

    We planned the eight-step booking walkthrough first, with the price and time adding up as you choose, then built the pages around it.

  3. 03

    Design system

    Black and white with a restrained red accent and strong, high-contrast headlines, for a bold and confident feel.

  4. 04

    The build

    Services with prices, durations and add-ons, barber profiles, a before-and-after slider, a 10-question FAQ and the booking flow with date and time rules, all written from scratch.

  5. 05

    Two languages and AI

    214 Greek text strings, and an AI assistant that knows the prices and suggests the right barber for a fade, a beard or a kid’s cut.

  6. 06

    Testing

    Checked in a real browser at 6 screen sizes from 375 to 1920 pixels, with a keyboard only, with animations switched off, and in Greek with an automatic check for any English left behind.

The numbers are measured from the demo’s own code and our test runs.

Fictional brand. Names, prices and details are invented, and no form on the demo sends anything.
